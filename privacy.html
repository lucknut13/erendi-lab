Erendi Lab privacy policy

Last updated: 3 November 2026.

The Erendi Lab app is a private tool. Only the owner of the YouTube channel @ErendiLab uses it. It is not offered to other users.

What the app accesses

The app uses the YouTube API Services with the owner's permission. It uploads videos to the owner's channel and reads the channel's own data and analytics. It does not access data of other Google users.

Storage and sharing

The access token stays on the owner's computer. The app does not sell, share, or send this data to third parties. The owner can revoke access at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.

Google and YouTube terms

By using the app, the owner agrees to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google processes data under the Google Privacy Policy.

Contact

Contact the owner through the Erendi Lab YouTube channel.
